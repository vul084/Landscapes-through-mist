Mount Zhong

Hu Yukun · 1660
From Views of Nanjing for Zhou Lianggong
Ink and color on paper

I was drawn to the layers of mountains and the way the mist leaves parts of the landscape hidden. For this draft, I focused on the tall central peak, dark pines, and the small pavilion.
